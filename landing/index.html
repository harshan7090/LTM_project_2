02 — Corrosion

Corrosion
isn't static.

It evolves. External corrosion is an electrochemical cell between pipe steel and soil — coating resists it, cathodic protection opposes it, and where both fail the wall thins until it cannot hold pressure.

Corvex models the rate, the uncertainty around it, and the consequence behind every segment.

Why is this segment high risk?

At 42% wall loss the Modified B31G evaluation returns ERF 0.91 — below the immediate-repair threshold, inside the scheduled band. The segment sits in a Class 3 location with a 612 ft impact radius, so it is an HCA and the reassessment interval is capped at 84 months.
