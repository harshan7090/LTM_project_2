01 / How it works

Five stages, one pipeline

A photograph and a row of asset data go in. A ranked work order for a named technician comes out.

01 of 05

Corrosion Vision

  1. 01

    Corrosion Vision

    Image-based corrosion detection. A vision model describes what is in the photograph and reasons over it alongside the asset data — as an indication, never as an input to the maths.

  2. 02

    Integrity Analytics

    Gradient-boosted corrosion rate with exact SHAP attribution, so every prediction says why — and a conformal interval says how far to trust it.

  3. 03

    Risk Engine

    Rate × consequence on the 5×5 the discipline already uses. HCA status under 49 CFR 192.903 / 195.450, impact radius, and remaining life from ASME B31.8 and B31G.

  4. 04

    Maintenance Intelligence

    Which inspection, which repair, and when — ranked by diagnostic value per crew-day, with the statutory cap applied last and only ever downward.

  5. 05

    Dispatch

    A constraint solver assigns the nearest qualified crew under skill, travel and deadline limits — and names the capacity gap when the fleet cannot be covered.

02 / Technology

Built to be defended

An engineer has to justify to a regulator why a segment got a DCVG survey and not an in-line run. A model that answers differently on Tuesday cannot be defended — so the decisions are code, and the language model only explains them.

Conformal prediction is the piece worth a second look. Quantile regression predicts a range; conformal guarantees it — distribution-free, finite-sample, any model. Measured on held-out data: 89.8% empirical coverage against a 90% target, and 93.9% against 95%. Intervals widen where the model is genuinely less certain. Conservative remaining-life uses the upper bound.
